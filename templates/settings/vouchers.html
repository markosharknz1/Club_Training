Voucher Rules

What your region's scheme is called, defaults for new vouchers, and the limits enforced when registering one. The defaults match the SA Sports Voucher program ($100, 10 sessions, 2 per child per calendar year) — other regions run the same idea under their own name and amounts (NSW Active Kids, QLD FairPlay Voucher, WA KidSport…). Changing these never touches vouchers already registered.
