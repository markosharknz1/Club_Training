Voucher Rules

Defaults for new vouchers and the limits enforced when registering one. The defaults match the SA Sports Voucher program ($100, 10 sessions, 2 per child per calendar year). Changing these never touches vouchers already registered.

A child can't register another voucher while they have this many with sessions remaining.
Unused sessions still carry over to the next year.
