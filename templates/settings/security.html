Security & Privacy

The database holds children's names, dates of birth and guardian contact details. Protecting it matters.

Protect the whole machine (do this today)

Turn on BitLocker / Device Encryption in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Device encryption. This encrypts the entire disk, so a stolen or lost laptop doesn't expose the club's data. It's built into Windows and free.

Coming soon: app-level encryption

A future update will add administrator passwords, database encryption at rest, an audit log, and a developer-held recovery key for when passwords are lost. Until then, full-disk encryption above is the protection that counts.
