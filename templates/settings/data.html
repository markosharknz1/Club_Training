Backups

The database is backed up automatically every day the app starts, to two places at once:

  • backups\ next to the app
  • Documents\Club_Training Backups\ — if your Documents folder is backed up by OneDrive, these copies also go to the cloud

The last 30 daily copies are kept in each location. To restore one, close the app and copy the backup file over badminton.db in the app folder.
