Email
Show email address fields on player records, imports and exports. Turn off if this club doesn't keep email addresses at all. Stored addresses are kept, just hidden.
Enables the Email page for announcements (tournaments etc.) and the provider settings below.
Used by default; you can switch per send on the Email page. Rough guide by volume: Gmail for occasional notes (~500/day cap), SMTP2GO for regular club emails (generous free tier), Mailgun for larger volumes.
Must be verified with SMTP2GO / Mailgun. Gmail always sends from the Gmail account itself.

Sends through Mailgun's API — suited to larger volumes. You need a Mailgun account with a verified sending domain. Leave the API key blank to keep the stored one.

{# ── Gmail ── #}
Gmail {% if gmail_password_set and gmail_username %}configured{% endif %}

Sends from a Gmail account (limit ≈500 recipients/day). Needs an App Password, not your normal password: turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google Account, then create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Leave it blank to keep the stored one.
