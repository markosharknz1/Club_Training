Coach Tracking

Coaches themselves are managed on the Coach Database page. This controls how much the app tracks about them. Switching modes never deletes anything — data is hidden, not destroyed, and comes back if you switch again.

Backfill pay for old markings (optional)

Sessions marked before a coach had a rate were recorded at $0. This applies each coach's current rate to those old markings from a date you choose. The result is an estimate — it assumes today's rate applied back then. Markings that already have an amount are never touched.
